What's inside.

Servers, chat, and calls for when typing takes too long.

Servers and channels
Text and voice channels sorted into categories, plus rules and announcements. Public servers show up in the explorer, and you can set your own invite code.
Live chat
Replies, reactions, typing indicators and unread badges. You can edit or delete what you sent, and everything shows up as it happens.
Mentions
Mentions autocomplete, get highlighted, and send a desktop alert, so you won't miss one.
Sharing
Drag in or paste images, video, audio and files. GIF search and custom emoji are in there too.
Voice and video
Call one person with camera and screen share, or hop into a voice channel with a few friends.
Your look
Set your avatar, banner, name colour and status. Servers can have their own theme, and you can build your own from two colours.

DMs only you can read.

DMs get encrypted in your browser before they're sent. The server only stores scrambled data it can't open.

For the curious: ECDH P-256 key exchange, AES-256-GCM, safety numbers to check a chat is really with who you think, and encryption at rest.
