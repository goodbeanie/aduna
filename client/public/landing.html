Everything you need

A home for every conversation.

Build a server, split it into channels, and bring your people in with a custom invite code.

#

Servers & channels

Categories, text and voice channels, rules and announcements. Public servers show up in the explorer.

💬

Live chat

Replies, reactions, typing indicators, unread badges, edits and deletes. Everything arrives in real time.

@

@mentions

Autocomplete, highlights, gold badges and desktop alerts, so nobody misses a ping.

🖼️

Share anything

Images, video, audio and files by drag and drop or paste, plus GIF search and your own custom emoji.

📞

Voice & video

1:1 calls with camera and screen share, and voice channels for small groups.

🎨

Make it yours

Your avatar, banner, name colour and status. Your server can have its own theme too.

Private by design

DMs only you can read.

Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted in your browser. The server stores ciphertext it can't open, and everything is encrypted again at rest.
