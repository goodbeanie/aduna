Features

What's inside.

Servers with channels, a chat that keeps up, and calls for when typing is too slow.

Servers and channels
Categories, text and voice channels, rules and announcements. Public servers show up in the explorer, and a custom invite code brings people in.
Live chat
Replies, reactions, typing indicators, unread badges, edits and deletes, all in real time.
Mentions
Autocomplete, highlights and desktop alerts, so a ping doesn't get lost.
Sharing
Drag in or paste images, video, audio and files. There is GIF search and custom emoji too.
Voice and video
One-to-one calls with camera and screen share, and voice channels for small groups.
Your look
Your own avatar, banner, name colour and status. A server can have its own theme as well.
Private by design

DMs only you can read.

Direct messages are encrypted in your browser before they leave it. The server only ever holds ciphertext it can't open.

ECDH P-256 key exchange, AES-256-GCM, safety numbers to verify a chat, and encryption at rest.
